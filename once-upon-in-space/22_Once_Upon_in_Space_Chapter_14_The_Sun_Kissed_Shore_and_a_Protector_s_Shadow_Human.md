#### **Once Upon in Space \- Chapter 14: The Sun-Kissed Shore and a Protector's Shadow**

The *Star-Hopper* emerged from its latest wormhole jump (now so smooth, thanks to the Hylidaean Chorus's harmonic attunement, it was like, totally first class) into a system dominated by a starkly beautiful, tidally locked planet. One side blazed under the constant, fierce glare of its sun, a panorama of scorched deserts and shimmering, heat-hazed oceans. The other side was a realm of perpetual twilight and deep, glacial ice.  
*"Okay, so, binary choice of vacation spots, team\!"* I announced, my voice echoing on the bridge. *"We've got 'Sizzle Central' or 'Frosty Fun Land.' And, get this – Unit 734 and I are detecting faint, almost identical beacon signals emanating from, like, both sides\! How curious is that?"*  
The decision was made to split into two teams. K'tharr, ever the pragmatist, noted, *"Two signals, two teams. Logical. We cover more ground, share the… unique environmental experiences."* His frills twitched, indicating what I was pretty sure was Xylothi understatement.  
Unit 734 and I quickly established a long-range quantum entanglement communicator – a little something whipped up from combined K'rell theory and some of Ray Toshlyra’s more esoteric communication principles I'd recently "remembered." This meant the teams could, like, totally stay in touch across the planetary divide.  
**Team Hot**, heading to the sun-blasted side, consisted of Cathy, Prysha (whose Elaran physiology, with her blonde hair and dark orange-ish resilient skin, was surprisingly well-suited to the heat), and me, Glitter, naturally, via Cathy’s visor. **Team Cold**, destined for the icy wastes, comprised K'tharr, Roric (who grumbled about missing out on potential "sun-baked treasure" but was secretly intrigued by the challenge), and Unit 734, whose K'rell adaptability made them indifferent to temperature extremes.  
The rest of the crew – Xylia, Grr'nak, the other Xylothi and K'rell, and the entire Hylidaean Chorus – would remain on the *Star-Hopper* in synchronous orbit, acting as a central command and, like, holding down the fort.  
Landing on the hot side was like stepping into a furnace, but a beautiful one. Vast deserts of crimson sand swept down to meet turquoise oceans that steamed gently under the relentless sun. The air was dry, hot, but breathable.  
Prysha, her emerald and blue eyes scanning their surroundings with a warrior's focus, immediately took charge of Cathy's well-being. "The star here is… intense," she stated, her usual tsundere sharpness softened by a clear concern as she watched Cathy already starting to glisten with sweat. "You will stay in my shadow, Star-Fallen. Or the shadow of these rock formations. No arguments."  
And so began their trek towards the beacon. It was, like, part expedition, part unexpectedly adorable bonding experience. Prysha was a constant, caring presence. She'd find the narrow strips of shade cast by towering, wind-sculpted hoodoos, gently guiding Cathy into them. When they reached a stretch of open desert, Prysha would unfurl a shimmery, heat-reflective Elaran cloak, holding it up to shield Cathy, their shoulders brushing. *"Don't get any ideas, it's just… tactically sound to protect the mission leader,"* Prysha would grumble, her cheeks a shade darker than usual, though she couldn't quite hide the soft smile playing on her lips when Cathy would offer a grateful, teasing remark.  
*"Aww, Prysha, you're like my own personal, super-fierce parasol\!"* Cathy giggled once, ducking under the cloak. Prysha just huffed, but her emerald eye glinted with affection. *"Just don't melt, Toshlyra. Reconstituting you would be… problematic."*  
Before they got down to serious beacon-hunting, they took what I dubbed a "mini-vacay" by a stunning, palm-fringed oasis leading to a warm, salty ocean. Cathy, laughing, splashed in the shallows while Prysha, ever vigilant but clearly enjoying the respite, taught her how to skip the flat, iridescent stones found along the shoreline. They shared rations under the shade of alien palm trees, teasing each other playfully. Prysha even tried to teach Cathy an Elaran warrior chant, which mostly ended up with both of them in fits of giggles at Cathy's mangled pronunciation. It was, like, a perfect little pocket of peace in their universe-spanning quest.  
*"You know,"* Cathy said softly one evening, as they watched twin moons rise over the shimmering ocean, the heat of the day finally mellowing. *"This is… nice. Even with the killer sun. Thanks, Prysha. For… everything."* Prysha just nudged her shoulder, a rare, unguarded smile gracing her lips. *"Someone has to make sure our last hope for humanity doesn't turn into a sun-dried tomato, right?"*  
The beacon, when they finally reached it after several more days of trekking (and cute, protective Prysha-moments), was hidden deep within a network of geothermal caves near the ocean. It was ancient, crystalline, and pulsed with the same familiar energy as the one that had revealed my, Glitter's, maternal connection to Cathy. This one, however, seemed to be only half of a message, a complex data-string that ended abruptly.  
*"Okay, Team Hot has secured Package A\!"* I transmitted back to the *Star-Hopper* and Team Cold. *"It's, like, definitely part of something bigger. Your turn, Frosty Friends\! Don't get too chilly\!"*
