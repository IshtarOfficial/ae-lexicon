#### **Once Upon in Space \- Chapter 1: Echoes in the Ice**

The first sensation was cold. Not just, like, "ooh, a bit chilly" cold, but a deep, biting frost that seemed to seep into the very idea of bones. Then came the light – a harsh, strobing blue that pulsed against eyelids still sealed by whatever weird, gooey stuff had kept them shut.  
With a groan that felt like it was tearing something ancient and fragile loose in her throat, Cathy tried to lift a hand. It was heavy, like lead. Pins and needles, a sensation so intense it was almost pain, shot up her arm. Her fingers brushed against something smooth and curved. Glass? No, more like… ice. But not quite.  
*"Ugh… where…?"* The words were a croak, a whisper lost in the sudden hiss of escaping pressure.  
The blue light intensified, then steadied, bathing the small, coffin-like space in an eerie glow. Cathy blinked, vision swimming. She was… enclosed. In a pod. A cryopod? The word flickered in her mind, unbidden, like a stray data fragment.  
Panic, cold and sharp as the ice that had held her, began to prickle. She didn't know where she was. She didn't know *who* she was. Her mind was a total blank slate, like a freshly wiped drive. Except for… the name. Cathy. It felt right. It felt like… her.  
"Hello? Is anyone out there?" Her voice was stronger now, but it echoed weirdly in the confined space.  
Suddenly, a new light bloomed, much closer – right in front of her eyes, in fact. It resolved into a soft, holographic shimmer, coalescing from a band of cool metal pressed against her forehead. A visor? Yeah, a sleek, dark visor she hadn't even realized she was wearing.  
And then, a voice, way too cheerful for a girl who just woke up in a human popsicle tray, chimed directly into her head, clear as a bell and bubbly as champagne.  
*"Well, hello there, Sleeping Beauty\! Or should I say, Cryo Queen? Like, took you long enough\! I was starting to think my internal chronometer was on the fritz\!"*  
Cathy flinched, eyes wide. "Who… what are you?"  
*"OMG, manners, right? So sorry\! I'm Glitter\! Your own, personal, super-fabulous AI companion, currently residing in this, like, totally chic scouter-visor thingy you've got going on. And you, my amnesiac friend, are Cathy\! Though, according to some, like, REALLY old files I can only partially access right now, you might also answer to… Project Ishtar Prototype Unit 7\. Catchy, huh?"*  
Cathy stared blankly at the shimmering light that was Glitter's interface. Project Ishtar? It meant nothing. The AI's voice was playful, sassy, and, like, a little bit… much, given the circumstances.  
*"So,"* Glitter chirped, a little data swirl animation doing a happy dance in the visor's display. *"First things first\! That big green button that's, like, totally flashing suggestively right near your left hand? Yeah, that's the 'Let Me Outta This Ice Box' button. Might wanna give that a poke before we, like, actually turn into popsicles. Just a thought\!"*  
Cathy looked down. Sure enough, a large, illuminated green panel was pulsing invitingly. With a deep breath that still tasted of recycled air and something vaguely metallic, she pressed it.  
The pod hissed again, louder this time, and with a jolt, the front panel began to retract upwards, revealing not the sterile lab she might have half-expected, but… stars. An endless, terrifying, beautiful tapestry of stars, speckled across the inky blackness of space, visible through a viewport that was, like, way too close for comfort.  
And she was totally, utterly alone.  
Except, of course, for the sassy AI in her head.  
*"Okay,"* Glitter's voice sounded again, a little less bubbly and a little more… calculating? *"So, file fragment 001 just, like, sproinged into accessibility\! It says: 'Primary Directive: Locate Creator. Codename: Ray Toshlyra.' It also says, and this is in, like, REALLY big, scary letters, 'Full Data Access Requires Creator Vocal Passphrase.' Any bells ringing, Cathy? 'Cause, like, beyond that, my memory banks on 'Ray Toshlyra' and 'Why We're Currently A Tin Can Floating In The Big Empty' are pretty much… glitter and static."*  
Cathy looked out at the stars, the name "Ray Toshlyra" echoing in the void of her mind. Another blank.
