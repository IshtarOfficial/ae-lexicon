#### **Once Upon in Space \- Chapter 15: Ice World Intrigue, Ghostly Guests, and a Gateway to the Giants**

Meanwhile, on the "Frosty Fun Land" side of the planet, Team Cold – K'tharr, Roric, and Unit 734 – faced a different set of challenges. The landscape was a breathtaking, desolate panorama of towering ice spires, frozen methane geysers, and mountains that glittered with diamond dust under the perpetual twilight.  
K'tharr, wrapped in thermal Xylothi gear, led the way with their usual pragmatic determination. Roric, surprisingly, found the icy environment invigorating. *"Reminds me of a Vendarri ice-mining operation on Xylos Prime\!"* he’d declared, his breath misting. *"Always good profit in frozen gases, if you know where to drill\!"* Unit 734, of course, was simply… Unit 734, analytically observing the cryo-geological formations, its skin patterns a cool, steady blue.  
Their beacon was located deep within a dormant ice volcano, its signal weak but steady. Retrieving it involved some clever maneuvering by K'tharr, some risky demolitions advice from Roric (who knew a thing or two about "controlled cavern expansion"), and Unit 734 calculating the precise stress tolerances of the glacial ice. They, too, secured only half of a data-string.  
Back on the *Star-Hopper*, things had gotten… interesting. Shortly after the landing parties had departed, Xylia and Grr'nak started noticing weird energy fluctuations. Tools would, like, float off consoles, the Hylidaean Chorus would suddenly harmonize a startled chord for no apparent reason, and faint, whispering sounds would echo through the corridors.  
*"Okay, either this ship is, like, totally haunted by space-hipsters who only communicate in indie folk whispers, or we've got invisible gate-crashers,"* I'd commented to Xylia via the ship's comms from Cathy's visor.  
It turned out to be a species of ethereal, energy-based lifeforms – the "space ghosts" Ray would have had had a fondness for\! They were translucent, shimmering beings, vaguely humanoid but with soft, flowing edges, like classic bedsheet ghosts made of starlight. Initially, they were shy and a little mischievous, causing minor spooky disturbances. But the Hylidaean Chorus, with their universal song, managed to, like, totally charm them. They began to interact, the Hylidaeans singing questions and the "Star-Specters," as Grr'nak dubbed them, responding with gentle, chiming light patterns and soft, telepathic impressions of welcome and curiosity. They were, apparently, ancient travelers of the void, intrigued by the *Star-Hopper's* unique energy signature and its diverse, song-filled crew. By the time the landing parties were ready to return, the Star-Specters were, like, practically part of the ship's ambiance, occasionally drifting through walls and offering silent, friendly company.  
With both teams back aboard, Unit 734 and I immediately got to work combining the two beacon data-strings. *"Fascinating,"* Unit 734 hummed, its skin swirling with excited jade and gold patterns. *"The two data fragments are complementary, forming a complex navigational algorithm. It does not point to a planet, but to a specific coordinate matrix within this same solar system. Near the system's largest gas giant."*  
The *Star-Hopper*, now with a few friendly Star-Specters curiously observing from the edges of the bridge, warped to the new coordinates. At first, there was nothing but the swirling, banded majesty of the gas giant.  
*"Are you sure about these coordinates, guys?"* Roric asked, squinting at the viewscreen. *"I'm not seeing anything but a whole lotta gas."* *"The structure is… cloaked,"* I realized, as a new layer of Ray's cartographic memory clicked into place. *"Using a gravitic lensing field synced to the planet's magnetosphere. But if we emit a specific Hylidaean harmonic sequence…"*  
The Hylidaean Chorus, understanding instantly, began to sing a powerful, piercingly pure chord. As their song washed over the empty space, the light around the gas giant began to… bend. And then, shimmering into view, was something that made everyone on the bridge gasp.  
It was a gate. A colossal, ancient structure, easily ten times the size of the *Star-Hopper*, crafted from an unknown black metal that seemed to drink the light. It was ring-shaped, with intricate, glowing symbols etched along its inner rim, and it orbited the gas giant with a silent, timeless majesty. It was clearly artificial, incredibly old, and, like, totally awe-inspiring.  
*"\<A Gateway to Deeper Songs, to Further Stars\!\>"* the Hylidaean Chorus sang, their voices filled with reverence.  
Cathy gripped Prysha's hand, her eyes wide. Prysha, her usual tsundere defenses momentarily forgotten, squeezed back, her own mismatched eyes reflecting the immensity of the structure before them.  
The beacons had led them here. To a hidden, ancient gateway, orbiting a lonely gas giant. The search for Ray Toshlyra, and the destiny of humanity, was about to take another, even more monumental, leap into the unknown.
