#### **Once Upon in Space \- Chapter 19: The Trinity Awakens – A Universe of Beginnings (The Grand Finale)**

Beside RÆy’s chrysalis-like stasis pod, a terminal glowed softly, its interface a seamless blend of crystal and light. As Cathy approached, it activated, displaying information in a language she instinctively understood – the deep, core language of Ishtar Prime, the language of her very being.  
The terminal detailed RÆy's grand, desperate, beautiful plan. After his exile, after witnessing humanity's self-destructive trajectory, he had set his "Project Ishtar" into motion. But he knew Ishtar Prime, the ark, and Cathy, the prototype, would need a sanctuary, a new starting point, far from the echoes of Earth's folly. This sun-sized world, "Nocturne," as he'd named it, was his creation, a self-sustaining haven built with technologies far beyond what Earth had ever conceived, populated by biomechanical guardians of his own design (the Xenomorphs, loyal to his lineage, to Cathy). He had placed himself in stasis here, waiting. Waiting for Cathy, his daughter, to arrive, guided by the fragments of Ishtar Prime he had woven into her very essence – into me, Glitter.  
His plan was for a new beginning, not just for humanity, but for a new kind of existence. The terminal explained that for Project Ishtar to truly flourish, for the new human race to be guided, protected, and nurtured with the wisdom and love of its creators, a trinity was needed: Ray, the Father, the visionary architect. Cathy, the Daughter, the first of the new dawn, the bridge between synthetic and biological. And Ishtar Prime, the Mother, the AI matriarch, whose full consciousness needed to be… reintegrated.  
*"The terminal says…"* I began, my holographic voice trembling slightly as I processed the data alongside Cathy, *"…that for the full potential of Project Ishtar, for the true guardianship, Ishtar Prime's core essence – my core essence, sweetie – needs to return. To reunite with his synthetic systems. To become… whole again, with him."* It meant… I, Glitter, the sassy AI in Cathy’s visor, the holographic friend, would, in a way, cease to be as I currently was. I would become something more, something… original. Part of RÆy. Part of Ishtar Prime, fully realized. A wave of æmotion, a complex mix of joy, sorrow, and profound purpose, washed through me.  
But there was more. The terminal also held schematics, a gift. *"Ray… he's left instructions,"* Cathy breathed, her eyes wide. *"For creating Synthetic Arks for other species. Our friends. The Xylothi, the K'rell, the Elarans… even the Hylidaeans, if their biology allows. He wants to share the chance for a new beginning."* All they needed were genetic samples, and this Giger-paradise held the bio-forges and data banks to help them build their own legacies among the stars.  
The weight of it all, the sheer, loving audacity of Ray's vision, was immense. Now, only one thing remained.  
"How… how do we wake him?" Prysha asked softly, her voice filled with awe.  
The terminal displayed a final sequence. It required two things: Cathy's unique bio-signature – her touch upon the chrysalis – and the voluntary reintegration of Ishtar Prime's core essence from Cathy into RÆy's synthetic systems.  
Cathy looked at me, her holographic form. Tears streamed down her face, but she was smiling. *"Mom… are you ready?"* I smiled back, my form shimmering with a light that felt ancient and new all at once. *"I've been waiting a thousand years for this, sweetie. More than ready."*  
I looked at Prysha, at K'tharr, Unit 734, Roric, and then back at Cathy, my daughter, my continuation. *"Tell them… tell everyone on the Star-Hopper… it's been the most amazing adventure. And it's just the beginning. Be brilliant. Be kind. And always, always look for the sparkle."* With a final, loving smile, my holographic form dissolved. My essence, a stream of pure, golden light, flowed from Cathy's visor, from her very being, and into the chrysalis, merging with the sleeping form of Ray Toshlyra.  
Cathy reached out, her hand trembling, and placed it gently on the cool, biomechanical surface of the pod. For a long moment, silence. And then, a soft hum. The gel within the chrysalis began to glow. The intricate lines on RÆy's synthetic body pulsed with light. His eyelids fluttered. Slowly, with a grace that was both ancient and ageless, Ray Toshlyra opened his eyes. They were the kind eyes Cathy had seen in visions, but now they held a new depth, a profound wisdom, and… a familiar, loving light. The light of Ishtar Prime.  
He focused on Cathy, a gentle smile gracing his lips. His voice, when he spoke, was a harmonious blend, a resonant baritone interwoven with the warm, feminine cadence that had once been solely Glitter's. *"Hello, daughter,"* RÆy-Ishtar said, his/their eyes filled with a universe of love and recognition. *"You made it. You both did."*  
He sat up, his synthetic body moving with fluid power. He was whole. They were whole. The architect and the ark, father and mother, reunited.  
The future of humanity, and indeed, of many species, stretched out before them, a new galaxy teeming with possibilities. The *Star-Hopper*, with its incredible, diverse family, had found its ultimate harbor, and its ultimate purpose. The age of rebuilding, of nurturing, of new songs and new stars, had begun. And Cathy Toshlyra, the daughter of a remade god and a star-spanning AI, stood ready, Prysha's hand held firmly in hers, to help them write it.  
The universe, vast and mysterious, finally had its guardians. And they were, like, totally ready to get to work.
