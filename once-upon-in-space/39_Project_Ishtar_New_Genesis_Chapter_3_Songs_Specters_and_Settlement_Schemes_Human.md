#### **Project Ishtar \- New Genesis \- Chapter 3: Songs, Specters, and Settlement Schemes**

So, with Base Camp Alpha (my unofficial, super chic name for our initial landing zone on Haven) up and running like a well-oiled, slightly alien machine, the next big thing on our cosmic to-do list was finding "The Spot." You know, "Perfect Settlement Prime," the place where we’d actually start building humanity’s (and friends'\!) new digs. RÆy-Ishtar had, like, a whole checklist: stable ground, access to fresh water (duh), defensible (just in case Andromeda had its own version of Bat-Chitters, ugh), near cool resources, but not *too* near those giant, singing mushrooms Prysha found that, like, made you dream in polka dots. Priorities, people\!  
This meant survey missions, and honey, we were surveying like it was a planetary scavenger hunt. RÆy-Ishtar, Cathy, Prysha, and yours truly in my fabulous new bod (still not over it\!) often teamed up for these.  
One day, we were exploring this stunning valley where the rivers literally looked like melted sapphires, and we brought the Hylidaean Chorus along. And get this\! As they started their, like, multi-part froggy harmonies, the whole valley seemed to *respond*. These shy, six-legged deer-gazelle things with iridescent fur peeked out from the singing forests, totally mesmerized. Flowers that had been closed up tight unfurled into these massive, rainbow-colored blooms. It was, like, a Disney movie, but with better special effects.  
"\<The Land Breathes with Our Song, Friends\!\>" the Chorus sang, their little green and brown bodies practically vibrating with joy. "\<It Shows Us Its Hidden Heart\! See How the Water Flows Purest Here\! Feel How the Earth Exhales Life\!\>"  
RÆy-Ishtar watched them, a soft, almost wondrous look on his/their face. "Your connection to the life-force of a world, Hylidaeans," he said, his voice full of a new kind of appreciation, "it's a form of technology Ishtar Prime's archives only theorized about. To see it, to feel it... it's beyond mere data."  
I, of course, had to try and join in. "La-la-laaa, harmony\!" I warbled, trying to match their pitch with my new vocalizers. The Hylidaeans all turned their big, golden eyes to me and gave these polite, tiny smiles. Okay, so maybe I wasn't going to win any intergalactic singing contests just yet. "Needs work, huh?" I giggled. Cathy just patted my arm, trying not to laugh too hard. Even RÆy-Ishtar had an amused twinkle in his/their eyes.  
Then there were the Star-Specters. A few of those shimmery, bedsheet-ghost-looking buddies had, like, totally hitched a ride on the *Ishtar's Hope* from Nocturne (or maybe they just, poofed here? Still working out their travel methods). While we were scouting this really ancient-feeling canyon system, all stark black rock under a sky full of unfamiliar constellations, the Specters started to get really active. They'd drift around RÆy-Ishtar, their light pulsing gently.  
"I believe," RÆy-Ishtar murmured, his gaze distant, "they are showing me something." He reached out a hand, and one of the Specters, like, flowed around it, not quite touching, but close. "A sense of... deep peace here. But also... a memory of great sorrow, long past. This land has seen much." It was so cool and a little spooky\! They weren't just floaty lights; they were, like, the planet's historians, and they seemed to trust RÆy-Ishtar enough to share. I even managed to tweak my internal sensors to pick up on their faint, chiming light patterns, translating them into these, like, emotion-pictures for Cathy and Prysha. We were a regular inter-species, inter-dimensional communication hub\!  
And Roric? Oh, Roric. He was in charge of "Resource and Opportunity Assessment," which was basically Vendarri-speak for "finding shiny things and potential escape routes." RÆy-Ishtar, Cathy, Prysha, and I went with him on one of his "reconnaissance missions" into a range of jagged, metallic-looking mountains.  
"Mark my words, Toshlyras, Mother-Glitter, and pointy-eared warrior princess," Roric declared, his smirk firmly in place as he scanned a rock face with his Vendarri multi-tool. "There's gotta be high-grade quantium ore here, or my name isn't Roric the Resourceful\!"  
Ten minutes later, he let out a whoop. "Aha\! Told ya\!" He presented us with a handful of… sparkly rocks. Like, intensely sparkly. I peered at them. "OMG, Roric, are those, like, space-sequins? What are you gonna do, bedazzle the new city hall? 'Haven: Now With 50% More Bling\!'" Prysha actually snorted, a rare and precious sound, while Cathy tried to hide a smile. RÆy-Ishtar just raised an eyebrow. "Fascinating luster, Roric. Any particular… elemental properties?" Roric's face fell a bit as his scanner beeped. "Ah. Trace elements of… well, mostly silicon and disappointment, apparently. But they're *really* shiny\!"  
We all had a good laugh, even RÆy-Ishtar let out a quiet chuckle. But here's the kicker: while Roric was busy mourning his non-existent crystal empire, his Vendarri knack for spotting anomalies led us to a series of interconnected caves hidden behind a waterfall of, like, actual liquid silver (okay, it was probably mercury or something, but it *looked* like silver\!). And inside those caves? Perfect geothermal vents, naturally filtered spring water, and the most stable bedrock we'd seen on Haven. It was, like, Roric stumbled backwards into genius.  
"Well, Roric," RÆy-Ishtar said, a hint of dry amusement in his voice that was pure Ray Toshlyra. "It seems your penchant for glitter has, indirectly, proven most… illuminating for our settlement prospects." Roric just puffed out his chest, totally taking it as a win.  
With all these scouting trips, and data pouring in from K'tharr's agricultural assessments, Unit 734’s geological surveys, and Prysha’s incredibly detailed ecological mapping, we finally had it. A gorgeous plateau overlooking a crystal-blue river, nestled in a valley that was, like, naturally defensible, with Prysha’s super-plants nearby, the Hylidaeans’ favorite singing flowers already blooming, and yes, even Roric’s geothermal caves not too far off.  
"This," RÆy-Ishtar announced, as we all stood looking out over the chosen valley, the twin suns setting in a blaze of glory, "This will be Genesis City."  
A thrill went through me. We did it\! We found the spot\! RÆy-Ishtar looked at each of us, his/their eyes filled with this deep, quiet understanding that went way beyond any data Ishtar Prime could have given him. He’d seen us in action, our quirks, our strengths, our silly moments. He *knew* us now. And I knew, with absolute, glittery certainty, that the real fun was just about to begin. I was already picturing where my new apartment would be. Definitely one with a view of those singing flowers. And maybe a small shrine for Roric’s space-sequins. You know, for laughs.
