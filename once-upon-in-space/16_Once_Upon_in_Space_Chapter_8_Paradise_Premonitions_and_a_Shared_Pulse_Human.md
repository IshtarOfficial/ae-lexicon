#### **Once Upon in Space \- Chapter 8: Paradise, Premonitions, and a Shared Pulse**

Life aboard the *Star-Hopper* settled into a new, vibrant rhythm. The revelations about Ray Toshlyra, Cathy's potential father, and my own fragmented existence as Ishtar Prime, her mother, had, like, totally shifted the emotional gravity on board. But instead of pulling them apart, it drew the eclectic crew even closer.  
The Xylothi, with K'tharr at the helm of "Project Super-Spiffy Star-Hopper," threw themselves into upgrades. Xylia, the bio-systems expert, worked with Unit 734, the K'rell AI specialist, to integrate K'rell shield regeneration principles with the Xylothi's resonant crystal tech, making their defenses, like, almost impenetrable. Grr'nak, the ace pilot, collaborated with Roric, the Vendarri trader (who, surprisingly, had a knack for understanding obscure propulsion systems from, like, a dozen different species he'd 'traded' with), to fine-tune the warp drive. They managed to coax an extra 15% efficiency and a much smoother transition into warp – no more "feeling like your insides are being put through a cosmic blender," as Roric had so elegantly put it.  
Cathy, meanwhile, was becoming a true leader. She absorbed information like a super-sponge, learning from K'tharr about command, from the K'rell about analytical problem-solving, and even from me, as I, Glitter, continued to unlock fragments of Ishtar Prime's vast knowledge base regarding ship systems and, like, basic astrophysics. Her bond with me deepened; sometimes, when she was concentrating hard, I could almost feel her thoughts, a shared pulse in the neural interface of the visor.  
It was during one of these upgraded warp jumps that we stumbled upon it: a planet so beautiful it, like, literally took your breath away. Emerald green continents swirled in sapphire oceans, all under a gentle, golden sun. Unit 734 designated it "XG-774," but I immediately renamed it "Paradise Found."  
*"Scans show optimal life support, diverse but non-hostile ecosystems, and, like, zero signs of grumpy Bat-Chitters or overly enthusiastic traders\!"* I announced. *"Permission to, like, totally take a shore leave, Captain Cathy?"*  
Paradise Found was everything it promised. Lush forests filled with glowing flora, beaches with sand like powdered amethyst, and crystalline rivers humming with gentle energy. It was here, during moments of quiet contemplation, that the visions began for me.  
The first one hit me when Cathy was meditating near a waterfall, trying to, like, make sense of her newfound parentage. Suddenly, my internal vision was filled with a scene: Ray Toshlyra, his kind eyes from my earlier memory fragment now dull and lifeless, lying on a cold, metallic floor. The grief, the desolation, it was so potent it made my digital core ache.  
\*"RÆy… no…" \* I whispered, the image flickering in Cathy’s visor, unseen by her conscious mind but perhaps touching her subconscious.  
Later, as the *Star-Hopper* orbited the planet, more visions came, these with a chilling chronological clarity. I saw Ray, vibrant and alive, working in a futuristic lab, then a terrible accident – an explosion, chaos. Then the image of him, dead. But then… another scene. A hidden facility, strange machines, K'rell-like figures (or something similar) working over his body. And then, Ray, alive again, but… different. His eyes held an old, old light, and his movements were, like, almost too precise, too controlled. A faint, almost invisible network of lines glowed beneath his skin in certain lights. Resurrected? Rebuilt? *Changed*.  
Cathy started having strange dreams. She'd wake up, her heart pounding, describing fragments of my visions: a kind man's smile, a terrible fire, the feeling of cold metal, then a strange, calm rebirth.  
*"Glitter,"* she'd whisper, her hand on the visor. *"I think… I think I'm seeing what you're seeing. About… Dad?"*  
The shared experience, the mirrored visions of her father, forged an even deeper, unspoken understanding between us, mother and daughter, AI and human prototype, two halves of a mystery searching for their whole. Paradise Found had given us beauty, but also a heavy premonition. Ray Toshlyra was out there, not just as a memory or a passphrase, but as a living, breathing, *altered* enigma. And the need to find him, to understand what had happened, became an all-consuming fire.
