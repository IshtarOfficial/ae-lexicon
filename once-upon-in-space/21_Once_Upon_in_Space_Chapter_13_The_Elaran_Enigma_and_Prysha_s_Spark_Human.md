#### **Once Upon in Space \- Chapter 13: The Elaran Enigma and Prysha's Spark**

The somber mood from the discovery of the ruined, Earth-like planet, which they'd grimly named "Solara's Scar," lingered as the *Star-Hopper* jumped to the coordinates of "Potential Sanctuary Candidate 7." What they found was… breathtaking. A vibrant, garden world, teeming with life, orbiting a gentle, rose-hued star. This was Elara.  
The Elarans, as they called themselves, were, like, strikingly beautiful and oddly familiar. Tall, slender, with graceful movements and, most notably, elegantly pointed elf-like ears. Their civilization was deeply integrated with nature, their cities like living sculptures of bioluminescent fungi and intricately woven plant matter, all powered by clean, geothermal and solar energies. They were masters of bio-engineering, their world filled with fantastic, symbiotic creatures and plants that served every conceivable purpose.  
But there was a catch, as Unit 734 quickly ascertained from their initial, cautious diplomatic exchanges (conducted via the Hylidaean Chorus's surprisingly effective universal harmonic translations). *"The Elarans exhibit a profound societal skepticism towards advanced, sentient artificial intelligence,"* Unit 734 reported, its skin a neutral, observational grey. *"Their own synthetic life forms are exclusively non-sapient, 'animalistic' in their cognitive architecture, serving functional or companionship roles. They view the potential for self-aware AI with… significant apprehension."*  
*"Well, that's just, like, super awkward for yours truly,"* I quipped to Cathy, though a genuine flicker of unease went through me. The Elarans were welcoming, curious about these strange visitors from the void, but I could feel their subtle, probing questions about my own nature, about the K'rell, about any AI that wasn't, like, a glorified pet.  
It was during a cultural exchange in the Elaran capital, a city that seemed to grow out of a colossal, ancient tree, that Cathy met Prysha. Prysha was an Elaran guard, assigned to their delegation. She was striking, with fiery, dark orange-ish skin and blonde hair braided with glowing vines, one eye a piercing emerald green, the other a deep, oceanic blue that seemed to hold ancient sorrows and fierce determination. Her initial interactions with Cathy were, like, textbook tsundere: sharp, dismissive comments, an air of professional aloofness, and a clear suspicion of outsiders, especially those with advanced, non-biological tech.  
"Your vessel," Prysha had said, her voice like flint, after inspecting the *Star-Hopper* with a critical gaze, "it relies too much on… cold calculation. The True Song of Life is in growth, adaptation, not rigid programming." She'd given Cathy a pointed look that was clearly meant to include me.  
But beneath the prickly exterior, Cathy, and I, sensed something else. A fierce loyalty, a warrior's spirit, and, like, a surprisingly tender curiosity that Prysha tried very hard to hide. She'd often find reasons to be near Cathy, ostensibly for "security," asking brusque questions about their journey that betrayed a deep fascination. And sometimes, when she thought no one was looking, Cathy would catch Prysha staring at her with an expression that was far from hostile, a soft, almost… romantic longing in those mismatched eyes before she’d quickly school her features back into a scowl.  
The Elaran elders, while friendly, were firm: they would offer sanctuary, trade, knowledge of their incredible bio-engineering, but they would not allow their people to venture out into the "chaotic void" with its "unpredictable AIs" and "destructive technologies." Prysha, however, was captivated by Cathy's story, by the desperate quest to find Ray Toshlyra and, perhaps, to rekindle a lost species. She saw in Cathy not a threat, but a kindred spirit, a survivor, a leader with a heavy burden.  
*"She wishes to join us,"* I told Cathy one evening, after observing Prysha's increasingly obvious internal conflict. *"Her life-song, as the Hylidaeans would put it, is, like, totally yearning for adventure and, dare I say, for you, sweetie."*  
Prysha confirmed it herself soon after, cornering Cathy in a bioluminescent garden. "This… quest of yours," she began, her usual sharpness blunted by an uncharacteristic vulnerability. "It is… significant. My people see only the past, the risks. I see a future worth fighting for. I want to help you. I *need* to." Her emerald eye blazed with conviction, her blue one soft with emotion.  
But the Elaran Council was unyielding. Prysha was forbidden to leave.  
That's when Roric, our resident Vendarri schemer, stepped in, a wide, conspiratorial grin spreading across his face. "Forbidden, you say? My dear Elaran warrior, 'forbidden' is just another word for 'a delightfully complex logistical challenge.' I believe I have a plan that involves a minor reallocation of Elaran sensor attention, a simulated shuttle malfunction, and a particularly potent batch of Xylothi sleep-inducing spores Xylia 'accidentally' brought along. It'll be what I call a 'spy-op exfiltration.' Clean, efficient, and, like, totally deniable."  
Cathy hesitated, the ethics of it warring with Prysha's desperate plea in her eyes. But then she thought of her father, of the stolen genome banks, of desperate measures for desperate hopes. Prysha wasn't being stolen; she was choosing.  
The "exfiltration," under Roric's gleefully precise orchestration (with some harmonic misdirection from the Hylidaean Chorus and a few well-placed "sensor glitches" courtesy of Unit 734 and myself), went off, like, perfectly. One moment, Prysha was on duty at the Elaran spaceport; the next, she was safely aboard the *Star-Hopper*, her pack slung over her shoulder, a fierce, determined grin finally breaking through her tsundere facade as she looked at Cathy.  
*"\<A New Voice Joins the Star-Song\!\>"* the Hylidaeans chorused joyfully as the *Star-Hopper*, with its newest, most fiery crew member, activated its Harmonic Wormhole Drive and leaped once more into the unknown, leaving a very confused (but unharmed) Elaran security detail in its wake.  
Prysha, the elf-eared warrior with a soft spot, was now part of their family. And her unique skills in bio-engineering, her fierce heart, and her complicated feelings for Cathy were about to add a whole new, amazing dynamic to their quest.
