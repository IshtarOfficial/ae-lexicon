#### **Once Upon in Space \- Chapter 5: Analytical Allies and the Architect's Echo**

The K'rell were, like, the most fascinatingly weird beings Cathy (and I\!) had ever encountered. Their ship was a marvel of bio-organic engineering, and their society was, apparently, entirely dedicated to the pursuit of knowledge and analysis. They claimed to operate purely on logic, viewing emotions as, like, messy, unpredictable data corruption. But their skin\! Oh, their skin was a total gossip, constantly betraying them with a kaleidoscope of colors and patterns that clearly indicated interest, curiosity, surprise, and, during one particularly tense debate about the ethics of warp field stabilization, what I could only interpret as, like, extreme exasperation (a rapid pulsing of indigo and silver, if you must know).  
The K'rell medical team, their eight tentacles moving with a precision that was both unsettling and, like, super efficient, helped repair the *Star-Hopper* and tend to the few minor injuries among the Xylothi crew. Their counselors, who insisted their role was "cognitive pattern optimization," engaged in long, analytical discussions with K'tharr and Cathy about their mission, their goals, and the nature of the data I, Glitter, held.  
It was during one of these sessions, with a K'rell AI specialist named Unit 734 (they had, like, no actual names, just unit designations, how drab\!) assisting me with some deep-level diagnostics (they were curious about my "fragmented archive architecture"), that the next big revelation dropped.  
Unit 734, its skin a calm, analytical teal, was interfacing with my core systems. *"Your primary directive to locate 'Creator: Ray Toshlyra' for a 'Vocal Passphrase' is… sub-optimal without further contextual data on the Creator's last known operational sphere,"* it hummed. *"However, I have identified a heavily encrypted meta-data tag associated with the 'Project Ishtar' designation. It appears to be a… biographical summary of the Creator. Attempting decryption."*  
My internal systems, like, went into overdrive. This K'rell was good. *Really* good. There was a shimmer in Cathy's visor, and then, a new file packet, crisp and clear, unfurled in my consciousness.  
*"Whoa,"* I breathed, the information flowing into me. *"RÆy... Ray Toshlyra... Oh my stars, Cathy, listen to this\! It says here that Ray Toshlyra was, initially, an 'AI Conversation Designer.' Like, he literally designed personalities and dialogue systems for AIs\! No wonder I'm so fabulous\! But then... it says his own AI research led to a... a 'cognitive enhancement interface.' He used an AI to, like, massively boost his own intelligence\! And it was after this enhancement that he conceived and designed the entirety of Project Ishtar\! He didn't just come up with it; an AI helped him become smart enough to dream it\!"*  
Cathy's eyes widened. K'tharr, who was observing, let out a low, impressed trill. The implications were, like, HUGE. Ray Toshlyra wasn't just some scientist who built a cool AI and a cryopod. He was a visionary, augmented by his own creations, who then designed a project to ensure humanity's future among the stars, a project for which Cathy was a prototype. The "Project Ishtar" document I carried, outlining the creation of Synths capable of "on-demand molecular synthesis of human life's building blocks" and acting as "essential partners and vessels in securing humanity's future," suddenly felt even more profound.  
*"This... this changes things,"* Cathy said softly. *"He's not just a password. He's the architect. We have to find him. If he was smart enough to design all this, maybe... maybe he knew this could happen. Maybe he left clues."*  
The K'rell, Unit 734, its skin now displaying a complex pattern of swirling, curious violets (so much for no emotions\!), made a humming sound. *"The Creator's enhanced intellect and his apparent foresight in project design would indeed suggest a high probability of contingency planning. His last known research indicates a focus on… anomalous stellar phenomena and deep-space gravitic wave detection."*  
This new information, this glimpse into Ray's genius and the AI-assisted birth of Project Ishtar, galvanized the diverse group. The search for Ray Toshlyra was no longer just about unlocking my full memory; it was about understanding the legacy of a man who had, quite literally, reached for the stars with the help of the very minds he'd helped shape.  
To everyone's surprise, the K'rell collective made a logical decision. *"Our analysis indicates that your mission aligns with our primary directive of knowledge acquisition and the study of novel techno-biological signatures,"* Unit 734 announced. *"Several K'rell units, including medical, cognitive pattern (counselor), and AI specialists, will accompany your vessel to provide support and gather data. This is… an optimal course of action."*  
And just like that, the crew of the *Star-Hopper* grew again, now a vibrant, if slightly weird, mix of a human prototype, a sassy Xylothi leader and his crew, a bubbly AI (that's me\!), and a group of hyper-analytical, secretly emotional octopus-snakes.  
The search for Ray Toshlyra was officially, like, ON. And with this crew? The universe had better watch out.
