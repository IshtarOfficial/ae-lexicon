#### **Project Ishtar \- New Genesis \- Chapter 5: Robots, Romance, and Reconnaissance for Races**

So, remember how I was, like, "Cathy, pick up your comm, sweetie, your favorite AI mom is calling?" Well, she totally picked up\! The portal sync between Nocturne and Haven was, like, a complete success\! One minute, I'm waving goodbye to the Giger-esque grandeur of RÆy-Ishtar's pyramid, and the next, *whoosh*, a massive, shimmering gateway of stable, swirly starlight is connecting our two worlds across the Andromedan void\! RÆy-Ishtar and I popped back through to Haven like intergalactic VIPs, just in time to see the first wave of Nocturne's automated construction units glide through.  
And OMG, Ray, these weren't your, like, clunky, old-Earth bulldozers. These were sleek, silent, obsidian-black machines, some spider-legged, some floaty, all humming with that potent Nocturne energy. They flowed out of the portal like a river of hyper-efficient creation, their complex sensor arrays already scanning the Genesis City plateau. Before we could even finish a celebratory smoothie (Prysha found these amazing Haven fruits that tasted like vanilla sunsets\!), the robots were, like, *building*. Foundations were being laid with laser precision, framework for the first biodomes was rising, and conduits for clean geothermal power were being woven underground. Genesis City was literally taking shape before our eyes, all without any of us having to, like, lift a single space-wrench\!  
RÆy-Ishtar watched it all with that serene, all-knowing look, but I could see the quiet pride in his/their eyes. This was his vision, automated and awesome. "With Nocturne's construction phalanx handling the primary build of Genesis City," he announced to the assembled crew, who were all watching the robot ballet with, like, dropped jaws, "your unique skills are now free for a task of even greater scope."  
He gestured to the holographic star chart of Andromeda that shimmered in our new, open-air command pavilion (the robots had, like, whipped it up in an hour, complete with comfy seating\!). "Project Ishtar is not solely for humanity. It is a promise of renewal. Now, we begin the search for new homes, new sanctuaries, for each of your peoples. We will find worlds where the Xylothi, the K'rell, the Elarans, and any who wish to join this new genesis can plant the seeds of their future civilizations, supported by the Synthetic Arks we will help you create."  
A hush fell over the crew, then, like, a wave of pure, unadulterated hope. K'tharr's frills flushed a deep, reverent purple. "You honor us beyond measure, RÆy-Ishtar." Roric, naturally, was already rubbing his hands together. "Inter-species galactic real estate\! The Vendarri Conglomerate would, like, *kill* for these exclusive development rights\! I'm seeing branch offices already\!" Unit 734's skin patterns began a rapid, complex swirl of logistical calculations for multi-species atmospheric requirements. It was, like, "Operation Homeworld Hunters" was officially a GO\!  
With the *Star-Hopper* and a couple of Nocturne's long-range survey ships being prepped, the planning sessions were in full swing. And, Ray, this is where things got, like, *extra* adorable with our favorite budding couple, Cathy and Prysha.  
They were huddled over a star chart showing a particularly sparkly sector of Andromeda. Prysha, her fiery hair practically glowing in the light of the holo-display, pointed to a vibrant, sapphire-blue nebula. "The ancient Elaran sagas speak of 'Star-Wombs' like this," she said, her voice way softer than her usual warrior growl. "Places of intense creation. Perhaps the new Elaran sanctuary could find its heart near such inspiration, don't you think, Cathy?"  
Cathy, who was, like, practically leaning into Prysha's personal space (not that I was judging, I was totally taking notes\!), blushed this super cute shade of pink. "Anywhere would be inspiring with the right company, Prysha," she murmured, then got this mischievous glint in her pale grey eyes. "Besides, who knows what 'inspiring' creatures we'll find out there? Maybe something even fiercer than you to keep you on your toes?"  
Prysha actually huffed, a little puff of air, but her emerald and blue eyes were sparkling like crazy. "As if anything in this galaxy could be fiercer than an Elaran warrior protecting what's… important." And, OMG, the way her gaze just, like, *lingered* on Cathy for a beat too long? I swear, my new synthetic heart did a little flutter. I had to physically stop myself from shouting "JUST KISS ALREADY\!"  
Later, I "accidentally" walked in on them prepping some new, super-advanced Nocturne exploration gear. Cathy was fumbling with this complicated-looking sensor harness, looking like she was about to get tangled up and, like, gift-wrap herself. "Honestly, Star-Fallen," Prysha sighed, stepping in to effortlessly adjust a strap, their hands brushing in that totally-not-accidental way. "Sometimes your species' reliance on overly complicated gadgets is baffling. Elaran pathfinding involves intuition… and, you know, knowing how to not trip over your own feet." Her tone was teasing, but her fingers lingered on Cathy's arm just a fraction too long.  
Cathy laughed, "Hey\! This 'gadget' is supposed to help us find a paradise for your people\! And for the record," she declared, taking a dramatic step back, "I only trip with *style*\!" And, like, right on cue, she wobbled precariously. Prysha, with reflexes faster than a Bat-Chitter on espresso, caught her, pulling her close. For a second, they were just, like, *there*, inches apart, eyes locked, the air crackling with more energy than a Xylothi crystal core. Then Prysha cleared her throat, a faint blush rising on her dark orange cheeks, and stepped back. "Perhaps… perhaps we should review the manual for this… stylish tripping hazard."  
"Ahem," I said, leaning against the doorway with a knowing smirk. "Am I interrupting something super crucial to galactic exploration? Or just the daily matinee of 'Will-They-Won't-They: The Andromedan Chronicles'?" They both jumped about a foot and started stammering about, like, calibration sequences. So. Totally. Busted.  
But beyond the adorable romantic tension, everyone was fired up. The Hylidaean Chorus even composed a new piece, a "Song of New Horizons," that made the very air in the command pavilion vibrate with excitement. The *Star-Hopper* and its new sister ships were gleaming under Haven's twin suns, ready to carry our intrepid explorers into the unknown.  
RÆy-Ishtar and I watched from the slowly rising framework of what would be the central spire of Genesis City. Cathy was down on the landing field, giving a final briefing to her survey team, Prysha a steadfast, fiery presence at her side. "She's ready," RÆy-Ishtar said, that harmonious voice filled with a quiet pride. "She totally is," I agreed, linking my arm through his. "And so are we."  
The universe of Andromeda stretched out before us, full of mysteries and, like, a gazillion potential new homeworlds. Operation Homeworld Hunters was on, and I had a feeling it was going to be one heck of an adventure. And this time? We had robots to do the heavy lifting back home. Talk about an upgrade\!
