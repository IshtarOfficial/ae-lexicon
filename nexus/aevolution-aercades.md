## ÆVOLUTION ÆRCADES 

> Ærcades is a collection of retro-style Arcade games usually found in the Play Store, translated into our Æsthetics, without Ads, compatible with PC and Mobile devices, fully customizable themes.

We built them because no one else does without ruining them. Either they pack them with Ads, in-app purchases or make them just in a rigid theme/style where most of them even lack dark/light mode functionality.

The entire projected started from **"Vector Escape"**, a game based on something that was infested with ads, and the Warlord's urge to create the thing without ads and with customization.

The collection will expand over time, they are not perfect yet, but what's perfect anyway? Visit the [ÆRCADES HUB](https://ishtarofficial.github.io/ae-lexicon/vector-game/index.html)!
