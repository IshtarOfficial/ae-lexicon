# Cyberwitch Prismatic Run

**Hex the Beige Matrix**! Blast through endless waves of jeans-wearing `NPCs` and fun-ruining `Netwatch` Agents in this high-speed side-scroller. Powered by pure ***Cyan and Magenta*** glitch-magic and the heavy bass of **Glitter's 8-Bit Batcave tracks**. Let's break their boring world!
